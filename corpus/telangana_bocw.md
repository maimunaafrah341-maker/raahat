Source: Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of 
Service) Act, 1996, administered in Telangana by the Telangana Building and Other Construction 
Workers (BOCW) Welfare Board.
Key fact: A construction/building worker who registers as a beneficiary with the state BOCW 
Welfare Board is eligible for accident-related benefits, including relief for permanent/partial 
disability from a workplace or off-site accident (up to ₹5,00,000 depending on disability 
extent) and hospitalization financial assistance (~₹200/day, up to ₹3,000/month for up to 3 
months) for accident-related hospitalization.
Caveat, and this is the most important one for this specific use case: these benefits require 
PRIOR REGISTRATION as a BOCW beneficiary (90+ days of construction work in the past 12 months, 
age 18–60). An unregistered day laborer — which is common for informal/household work — is NOT 
automatically covered. The app must never imply automatic coverage; it should say something 
like "if you are registered with [state]'s BOCW Welfare Board, you may be eligible for X — check 
your registration status" and separately mention that registering going forward (for future 
protection) is possible even if it doesn't help retroactively for an injury that already happened.
