UK sources say call 999; in India the ambulance number is 108.
Key fact: First aid for a cut-off (severed) finger or fingertip, while waiting for help or on the way to hospital:
- Call 108 for an ambulance, or get to a hospital emergency department straight away.
- Stop the bleeding: press firmly on the wound with a clean, non-fluffy cloth or dressing, and keep pressing.
- Wrap the cut-off part in cling film, or in gauze soaked in saline, and put it in a sealed plastic bag.
- Put that sealed bag into a container of ice and water ("slush"), so the part stays cold without touching the ice.
- Take the cut-off part to the hospital together with the injured person.
Caveat: What not to do, and limits of this advice:
- Do not put the cut-off part directly on ice or directly into ice water: ice on skin can cause damage.
- This is first aid only. It does not replace emergency medical care, and it cannot tell you whether a part can be reattached; only the hospital can.
