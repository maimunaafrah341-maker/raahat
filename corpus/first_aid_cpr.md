Source: St John Ambulance, "CPR" (https://www.sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/cpr/), for an adult who is unresponsive and not breathing normally. UK sources say call 999 or 112; in India the ambulance number is 108 (112 also works).
Key fact: If an adult has collapsed, does not respond and is not breathing normally:
- Call 108 for emergency help and start CPR straight away. If someone is with you, ask them to call while you start.
- Kneel beside them. Put the heel of one hand on the centre of their chest, put your other hand on top and interlock your fingers.
- Push down hard, about 5 to 6 centimetres, then let the chest come back up fully.
- Push at a rate of 100 to 120 times a minute.
- If you are not trained, or don't want to give rescue breaths, keep giving chest compressions without stopping.
- If someone can bring a defibrillator (AED), ask them to; if you are alone, don't leave the person to find one.
- Keep going until emergency help takes over, the person starts breathing normally, or you are too exhausted to continue.
Caveat: Limits of this advice:
- These steps are for adults. Babies and children need different CPR; the 108 call handler can guide you.
- This is first aid only. It does not replace emergency medical care.
