Source: NHS, "Heat exhaustion and heatstroke" (https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/heat-exhaustion-heatstroke/). UK sources say call 999; in India the ambulance number is 108.
Key fact: If someone is unwell from the heat:
- Move them to a cool place.
- Remove all unnecessary clothing like a jacket or socks.
- Give them plenty of water to drink.
- Cool their skin: spray or sponge them with cool water and fan them.
- Call 108 if they are still unwell after 30 minutes of resting in a cool place, being cooled and drinking fluids, or if they have a very high temperature, hot skin that is not sweating, a fast heartbeat, confusion and lack of coordination, a seizure or fit, or loss of consciousness.
Caveat: Limits of this advice:
- Heatstroke is an emergency: do not wait to see if it gets better if any of the signs above are there.
- This is first aid only. It does not replace emergency medical care.
