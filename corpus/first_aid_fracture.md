Source: British Red Cross, "Broken bones and fractures" (https://www.redcross.org.uk/first-aid/learn-first-aid/broken-bone); St John Ambulance, "Broken bones and fractures" (https://www.sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/fractures-and-broken-bones/). UK sources say call 999; in India the ambulance number is 108.
Key fact: First aid for a suspected broken bone:
- Support the injured part above and below the injury, and keep it still.
- Put soft padding, such as clothing or blankets, around it for support.
- Call 108 if the person cannot move by themselves or is in a lot of pain.
- Keep supporting the injury until help arrives.
Caveat: What not to do, and limits of this advice:
- Do not move the injured part or the person unless they are in immediate danger.
- Do not try to straighten the limb or force it into position.
- This is first aid only. It does not replace emergency medical care.
