Source: NHS, "Poisoning" (https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/poisoning/). UK sources say call 999; in India the ambulance number is 108.
Key fact: If someone may have swallowed, touched or breathed in something harmful:
- Call 108 or go to hospital straight away, especially if they are unconscious, not breathing, struggling to breathe or speak, or having a seizure (fit).
- If they are unresponsive and not breathing, start CPR.
- If they are unconscious but still breathing, put them in the recovery position.
- Look for what caused it, such as a bottle, packet or packaging.
- Take some of the substance or its packaging to show the doctors, and any medicines the person takes.
Caveat: Limits of this advice:
- Do not try to make them sick (vomit).
- Do not give them anything to eat or drink.
- This is first aid only. It does not replace emergency medical care.
