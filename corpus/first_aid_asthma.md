Source: NHS, "Asthma attack" (https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/asthma/asthma-attack/). UK sources say call 999; in India the ambulance number is 108.
Key fact: During an asthma attack:
- Sit up straight, to help breathing. Try to stay calm.
- With a blue reliever inhaler: take 1 puff every 30 to 60 seconds until you feel better, up to a maximum of 10 puffs. Shake the inhaler between puffs, and use a spacer if you have one.
- Call 108 if the person starts to feel worse at any time, does not feel better after the maximum dose, or does not have an inhaler.
- If they are not better after 10 minutes and the ambulance has not arrived, use the inhaler again, up to the same maximum.
Caveat: Limits of this advice:
- Do not drive yourself to the hospital during an asthma attack.
- Other inhalers (AIR or MART) have different doses: follow the person's own asthma plan.
- This is first aid only. It does not replace emergency medical care.
