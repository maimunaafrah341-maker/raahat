Key fact: First aid for suspected heart attack or severe chest pain:
- Call 108 straight away and say you think it is a heart attack.
- Sit the person down with their head and shoulders supported and knees bent.
- Give them one 300mg aspirin tablet to chew slowly, unless they are under 16 or allergic to aspirin.
- Keep checking on them until help arrives. If they become unresponsive and stop breathing normally, start CPR.
Caveat: Limits of this advice:
- This is first aid only. It does not replace emergency medical care.
