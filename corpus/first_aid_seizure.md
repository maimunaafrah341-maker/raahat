Source: NHS, "What to do if someone has a seizure (fit)" (https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/what-to-do-if-someone-has-a-seizure-fit/). UK sources say call 999; in India the ambulance number is 108.
Key fact: Helping someone who is having a seizure (fit):
- Only move them if they are in danger, such as near a busy road or a hot cooker.
- Cushion their head if they are on the ground.
- Loosen any tight clothing around their neck, such as a collar or tie, to help breathing.
- Note the time the seizure starts and finishes.
- When the shaking stops, turn them on to their side. Stay with them and talk to them calmly until they recover.
- Call 108 if it is their first seizure, if it lasts more than 5 minutes (or longer than is usual for them), if they do not regain full consciousness or have several seizures without waking, if they are seriously injured, or if they have difficulty breathing afterwards.
Caveat: Limits of this advice:
- Do not put anything in their mouth, including your fingers.
- Do not give them food or drink until they have fully recovered.
- This is first aid only. It does not replace emergency medical care.
