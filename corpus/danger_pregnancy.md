Source: NHS, "Pregnancy symptoms you need to get help for" (https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/common-symptoms/pregnancy-symptoms-you-need-to-get-help-for/). UK sources say call 999; in India the ambulance number is 108.
Key fact: During pregnancy, get medical help immediately for any of these:
- Bleeding from the vagina.
- A change in the baby's movements.
- Fluid leaking from the vagina, which may be a sign of labour.
- A severe headache, or sudden problems with vision (these can be signs of pre-eclampsia).
- Sudden swelling of the face, hands, legs or feet.
- Breathlessness that comes on suddenly.
- Pain in the tummy, pelvis or back.
- A high temperature (fever), which can be a sign of infection.
- Very severe vomiting.
Caveat: Limits of this advice:
- Call 108 for any life-threatening emergency, at any stage of pregnancy.
- This is general guidance, not a diagnosis. It does not replace a doctor, midwife or emergency care.
