Source: Paschim Banga Khet Mazdoor Samity & Ors v. State of West Bengal & Anor., (1996) 4 SCC 
37 / AIR 1996 SC 2426 — Supreme Court of India, May 6, 1996.
Key fact: A worker with severe injuries was turned away by multiple government hospitals for 
lack of beds/facilities. The Supreme Court held that Article 21 (Right to Life) imposes an 
obligation on the State to safeguard life, and that a government hospital's failure to provide 
timely emergency treatment violates this right — the State cannot use lack of financial 
resources as a defense. The Court ordered compensation and directed the state to plan for 
emergency care capacity.
