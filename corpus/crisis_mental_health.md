UK sources say call 999; in India the ambulance number is 108.
Key fact: If someone is thinking about suicide or harming themselves:
- If they have seriously harmed themselves, or may be about to, call 108 for an ambulance or go straight to a hospital emergency department.
- If you can't do that yourself, ask someone else to call 108 or take you.
- To talk to someone now, call Tele-MANAS on 14416: it is free, open 24 hours, and you can choose your language.
- In Hyderabad, Roshni also listens on 040 6620 2000 (Monday to Saturday, 11am to 9pm).
- If you are with someone who is struggling, try to get them to talk to you. Listening and taking them seriously can help more than having the answers.
Caveat: Limits of this advice:
- This is not a diagnosis or treatment. A mental health crisis needs care from a doctor, like any other emergency.
- If tablets, poison or anything else has been swallowed, treat it as poisoning and call 108 straight away.
