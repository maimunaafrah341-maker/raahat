Source: NHS, "Burns and scalds" (https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/burns-and-scalds/); British Red Cross, "Burns" (https://www.redcross.org.uk/first-aid/learn-first-aid/burns); St John Ambulance, "Burns and scalds" (https://www.sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/burns-and-scalds/). UK sources say call 999; in India the ambulance number is 108.
Key fact: First aid for burns and scalds:
- Cool the burn under cool running water for at least 20 minutes.
- Remove jewellery or clothing near the burn before the area swells, unless it is stuck to the skin.
- After cooling, cover the burn loosely with cling film laid over it lengthways, not wrapped around it.
- Keep the person warm with a blanket or clothing, but not on the burned area.
- For a serious burn, call 108 or go to a hospital emergency department.
Caveat: What not to do, and limits of this advice:
- Do not use ice, iced water, creams, or greasy things like butter on a burn.
- Do not pull off clothing that is stuck to the burn.
- This is first aid only. It does not replace emergency medical care.
