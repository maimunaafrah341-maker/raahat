These are secondary guides, not the scheme's own rules; the official source is the Aarogyasri Health Care Trust (aarogyasri.telangana.gov.in).
Key fact: How to use Aarogyasri at a hospital:
- Aarogyasri works only at empanelled (network) hospitals. Each one has an "Aarogya Mithra" help desk that checks whether you are covered.
- If you have an Aarogyasri health card: show it at the Aarogya Mithra desk, with the patient's Aadhaar.
- If you don't have the health card but your family has a white ration card: you may still be covered. Show the white ration card and the patient's Aadhaar at the Aarogya Mithra desk and ask them to check.
- If you have neither: ask the Aarogya Mithra desk whether your family is in the eligible list (it is checked through Aadhaar). Families who are eligible but not yet enrolled can enrol at a MeeSeva centre, a primary health centre or a government hospital, usually with the white ration card, Aadhaar, proof of residence and photographs.
- Get the health card made in advance if you can, instead of waiting for an emergency.
Caveat: Limits of this advice:
- Coverage depends on being eligible (generally families with a white ration card) and on the treatment being covered at that hospital; only the Aarogya Mithra desk or the scheme can confirm it.
- Not having a card never removes a registered hospital's duty to stabilise an emergency first; say this alongside it.
- Guides differ on the yearly limit (₹5 lakh or ₹10 lakh); don't state an amount unless asked, and then say it varies by source.
