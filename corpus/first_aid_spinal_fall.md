Source: St John Ambulance, "Spinal injury" (https://www.sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/spinal-injury/). UK sources say call 999; in India the ambulance number is 108.
Key fact: First aid after a fall from height, when the neck or back may be injured:
- Call 108, or ask someone to call for you.
- Do not move the person, and tell them not to move, unless they are in immediate danger.
- Keep their head, neck and spine in a straight line: kneel behind their head and hold it steady.
- If someone can help, place rolled-up blankets, towels or clothes on both sides of their head.
- Keep supporting their head until emergency help takes over.
Caveat: What not to do, and limits of this advice:
- If they are not breathing normally, do not tilt their head back. Put your fingertips at the angles of the jaw and gently lift the jaw to open the airway.
- This is first aid only. It does not replace emergency medical care.
