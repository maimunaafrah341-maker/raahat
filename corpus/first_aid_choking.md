Source: St John Ambulance, "Choking adult" (https://www.sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/choking/). UK sources say call 999 or 112; in India the ambulance number is 108 (112 also works).
Key fact: If an adult is choking:
- Ask them "are you choking?" If they can't breathe, cough or make any noise, they need help straight away.
- Cough it out: encourage them to cough, and remove any obvious obstruction from their mouth.
- Hit it out: if coughing doesn't work, give up to five sharp blows on their back, between the shoulder blades.
- Squeeze it out: if back blows don't clear it, give up to five abdominal thrusts.
- If the blockage has not cleared, call 108 straight away.
- Repeat five back blows and five abdominal thrusts until help arrives, checking their mouth each time.
Caveat: Limits of this advice:
- These steps are for adults. Babies under one year and young children need different first aid; the 108 call handler can guide you.
- If they become unresponsive, start CPR and call 108 if no one has yet.
- This is first aid only. It does not replace emergency medical care.
