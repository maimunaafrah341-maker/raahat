Key fact: After a dog, cat, monkey or other animal bite or scratch:
- Wash the wound straight away with plenty of soap and running water, for at least 15 minutes.
- If it is bleeding heavily, press a clean cloth firmly onto the wound.
- Go to a doctor or the nearest anti-rabies clinic or government hospital the same day for the anti-rabies vaccine, and complete the full course.
- Severe bites may also need rabies immunoglobulin (serum) with the vaccine; the doctor will decide.
Caveat: Limits of this advice:
- Do not put chilli, mustard oil, turmeric, lime, salt, soil, oil or plant juice on the wound.
- Do not cover the wound tightly or get it stitched unless a doctor decides to.
- Rabies is almost always fatal once symptoms start, so do not wait to see whether the animal stays well.
- This is first aid only. It does not replace medical care.
