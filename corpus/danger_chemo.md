Source: Macmillan Cancer Support, "Sepsis (blood poisoning)" (https://www.macmillan.org.uk/cancer-information-and-support/impacts-of-cancer/sepsis). UK sources say call 999; in India the ambulance number is 108.
Key fact: During cancer treatment such as chemotherapy, an infection can quickly become serious (sepsis). Call the cancer team's 24-hour helpline straight away if:
- The temperature goes over 37.5°C, or below 36°C.
- The person feels shivery, cold and unable to get warm, or feels less well than normal.
- Do not wait: sepsis is easier to treat if it is caught early.
- Call 108 if there is slurred speech or confusion, extreme shivering or muscle pain, no urine passed in a day, severe shortness of breath, skin that is mottled or discoloured, or it is the worst they have ever felt.
Caveat: Limits of this advice:
- This is general guidance, not a diagnosis. It does not replace the cancer team's advice or emergency care.
