Source: Telangana Rajiv Aarogyasri Scheme (implemented as "Ayushman Bharat PMJAY – Rajiv 
Aarogyasri" under the Aarogyasri Health Care Trust), Government of Telangana.
Key fact: Community health insurance for BPL (Below Poverty Line) families in Telangana. 
Provides cashless treatment for empanelled conditions at network/empanelled hospitals (currently 
covering hundreds of treatment categories, cap up to ₹10 lakh/year per the current scheme 
iteration). Beneficiaries need a BPL ration card / scheme enrollment.
Caveat: only applies at EMPANELLED hospitals, and only for BPL-registered families — this is 
the single most important thing to surface, since going to a non-empanelled hospital voids the 
benefit entirely. The app should always frame this as "check whether this specific facility is 
empanelled" rather than assume coverage.
