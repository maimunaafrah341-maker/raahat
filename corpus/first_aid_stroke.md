Source: NHS, "Stroke - Symptoms" (https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/stroke/symptoms/). UK sources say call 999; in India the ambulance number is 108.
Key fact: Signs of a stroke and what to do (the FAST test: face, arms, speech, time):
- Face: one side of the face may droop, and it may be hard to smile.
- Arms: the person may not be able to fully lift both arms.
- Speech: they may slur their words or sound confused.
- Time: call 108 straight away if you see any of these signs.
- Call 108 even if the signs stop after a short time, or if there were signs of a stroke in the last 24 hours that have now stopped.
Caveat: Limits of this advice:
- Do not drive yourself to hospital if you think you are having a stroke.
- This is first aid only. It does not replace emergency medical care.
