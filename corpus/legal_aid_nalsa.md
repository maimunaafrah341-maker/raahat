Source: National Legal Services Authority (NALSA), under the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, section
12 (https://nalsa.gov.in/promoting-inclusive-legal-system/).
Key fact: Free legal services (advice, and a lawyer for a case) are available from Legal Services
Authorities. NALSA's toll-free helpline is 15100. Those eligible include women and children, members of
Scheduled Castes or Scheduled Tribes, industrial workmen, persons with disability, persons in custody,
victims of trafficking, victims of a mass disaster, ethnic violence, caste atrocity, flood, drought,
earthquake or industrial disaster, and persons with an annual income of less than ₹1,00,000 or as notified
by the Central or State Government.
Caveat: Limits of this advice: the income limit can differ by state; the District or State Legal Services
Authority decides who qualifies. Legal aid helps the person decide and file; it does not decide the case.
Use this when the person wants to complain, claim compensation or go to court about a hospital and worries
about the cost of a lawyer.
