Source: The Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act, 2010 (Central Act No. 23 
of 2010), adopted by Telangana in 2011 as the Telangana State Clinical Establishments 
(Registration and Regulation) Rules, 2011.
Key fact: Section 12(2) requires every registered clinical establishment to provide the medical 
examination and treatment necessary to stabilize a person's emergency medical condition, within 
the staff and facilities available — before any referral or transfer — regardless of the 
patient's ability to pay immediately. This applies to registered establishments in Telangana, 
including government hospitals.
Caveat to preserve in the app: this only obligates STABILIZATION, not free full treatment — a 
hospital can still bill for care beyond stabilization, and can still transfer a patient to 
another facility once stabilized.
